Our Clinical Services

Comprehensive primary healthcare for individuals and families — all under one roof in Rosedale, Uitenhage.

✚ Walk-ins Welcome. No appointment necessary for most services. For assessments (PDP, Disability Grant, Pre-Marital), please call ahead to confirm availability. Free Blood Pressure checks are available during all operating hours.

22 Services Available

What We Offer

We provide a wide range of evidence-based primary care services to meet the diverse health needs of our patients and community.

1

Medical Consultation & Care Plan

Comprehensive consultations for adults and children, including personalised care planning.

2

Management of Common Medical Conditions

Diagnosis and treatment of everyday illnesses, infections, and acute health complaints.

3

Hypertension, Diabetes, HIV & TB Management

Ongoing care and monitoring for patients living with chronic conditions.

4

Management of Sexually Transmitted Infections

Confidential screening, diagnosis, and treatment of STIs.

5

Medical Check-Up

General health assessments and wellness screenings for all age groups.

6

HIV Counselling & Testing

Voluntary, confidential HIV testing with pre- and post-test counselling.

7

Antenatal Care

Comprehensive care for pregnant women throughout pregnancy — monitoring, advice, and support.

8

Ultrasound Scan (Sonar)

In-practice diagnostic ultrasound imaging available on-site.

9

Men's Clinic

Dedicated services addressing men's health needs including screening, counselling, and treatment.

10

Family Planning & Contraception

Counselling and access to a full range of contraceptive options.

11

Women's Clinic

Specialised services for women's health including gynaecological care and wellness.

12

Male Medical Circumcision

Safe, clinical circumcision services performed by our registered medical doctor.

13

Professional Driving Permit (PDP) Assessment

DLTC-recognised medical fitness assessments for PDP applications and renewals.

14

Disability Grant Assessment (SASSA)

Medico-legal assessments to support SASSA disability grant applications.

15

Pre-Marital Consultation

Health assessments and counselling for couples preparing for marriage.

16

Medical Fitness Assessment

Occupational and general fitness-to-work or fitness-to-participate certificates.

17

Minor Clinical Procedures

In-practice procedures including wound care, suturing, and minor excisions.

18

PrEP & PEP for HIV

Pre-Exposure and Post-Exposure Prophylaxis to prevent HIV infection.

19

Pregnancy, Glucose, HIV & Urine Tests

On-site rapid testing for a range of common health indicators.

20

PAP Smear for Cervical Cancer

Cervical cancer screening for eligible women — early detection saves lives.

21

Blood Pressure Check

Free blood pressure measurement available during all operating hours. No appointment needed.

22

Virtual Consultation

Telephone or video consultations available — speak to our doctor remotely when you can't come in.

Need to See Us?

Walk in during our operating hours or contact us to arrange an appointment for any of the above services.
