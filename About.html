Our Doctor

Meet Dr I. Irene

Dr I. Irene is a fully registered general medical practitioner with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA). Holding an MBBS from the University of Benin and an MSc (Med) with UTC, Dr Irene brings a breadth of knowledge and a genuine commitment to patient care.

With qualifications spanning medicine and healthcare management, Dr Irene is equipped to manage a wide range of primary care conditions — from acute illnesses to chronic disease management, antenatal care, occupational health assessments, and more.

At Baird Medical Centre, every patient is seen as an individual. Dr Irene takes the time to listen, assess, and tailor each care plan to the patient's unique needs — because good medicine is personal medicine.

Baird Medical Centre is registered and regulated by the HPCSA, ensuring that all services meet the highest standards of clinical practice and professional ethics.

Our Mission
