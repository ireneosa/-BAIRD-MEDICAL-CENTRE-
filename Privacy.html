Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Baird Medical Centre collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and applicable HPCSA guidelines.

1. Who We Are

Baird Medical Centre is a registered general medical practice located at 146 Acacia Street, Rosedale, Uitenhage, Eastern Cape. We are a responsible party as defined under POPIA and are committed to protecting the personal information of all patients and visitors.

Contact: ikponmwosai185@gmail.com | 063 004 4 | 041 988 111

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information only where it is necessary for providing healthcare services. This may include:

  • Full name, date of birth, gender, and identity number
  • Contact details including address, phone number, and email address
  • Medical history, current medications, allergies, and clinical notes
  • Medical aid or health insurance information
  • Information shared during consultations, assessments, and treatments
  • Referral or previous healthcare provider information

We also collect information when you contact us by phone, WhatsApp, or email for the purpose of scheduling appointments.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Providing clinical care, diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up
  • Maintaining accurate patient health records
  • Processing medical aid claims and billing
  • Communicating appointment reminders and health-related information
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations (HPCSA, POPIA)
  • Referral to other healthcare providers where clinically necessary

4. Confidentiality of Health Records

All health information is treated as strictly confidential. Clinical records are maintained securely and are only accessible to authorised personnel directly involved in your care. We do not share your health information with third parties without your explicit consent, except where required by law (e.g. notifiable medical conditions, court orders).

5. Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Patient records are stored securely and retained in accordance with HPCSA guidelines (minimum six years from the date of last entry or until the patient turns 21 years of age, whichever is later).

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With other healthcare providers for the purpose of your ongoing care (with your consent)
  • With medical aid schemes for billing purposes
  • As required by law, regulation, or court order
  • To report notifiable conditions to public health authorities

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information held by us
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at ikponmwosai185@gmail.com.

8. Cookies and Website Analytics

This website may use basic analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with our content (e.g. pages visited, device type). This data is anonymous and aggregated. No personally identifiable information is collected through website analytics. You may disable cookies through your browser settings.

9. Children's Information

We provide healthcare services to patients of all ages. When treating minors, we collect only the information necessary for clinical care, and consent is obtained from a parent or guardian as required by law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legislation or practice. The most current version will always be available on this website. Significant changes will be communicated to registered patients.
